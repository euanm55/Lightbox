Series

    Drop a zip of DICOM files here

    MRI, CT, X-ray, ultrasound. A whole study exported from a hospital portal or CD works as-is, folders and all. Everything stays in this browser tab; nothing is uploaded anywhere.

    Then scroll through slices, set window/level, and use Share with an AI on the right to export a contact sheet Claude or ChatGPT can read.

    Window / level

    — —

    Drag on the image: left–right changes width, up–down changes centre. Wheel scrolls slices, Ctrl+wheel zooms.

    Share with an AI

    Claude and ChatGPT read ordinary images, not DICOM. Export a labelled contact sheet of this series, then paste the image and the study summary into the chat.

    For Claude Code or ChatGPT's code interpreter, the repository's tools/dicom_export.py produces the same pack from the original zip.
